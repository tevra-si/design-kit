# Focused implementation guide

Use an evidence ledger: claim, source, confidence, design consequence. Screenshots establish visible facts, not task success or performance. Inspect content length, localization, privacy constraints and accessible recovery. Prefer existing product evidence over generic catalogs. Search categories include product, ux, color, typography, layout, motion and industry. Research outputs should distinguish a recommendation from a measured outcome. Do not import an upstream palette or font asset without checking its own license.
